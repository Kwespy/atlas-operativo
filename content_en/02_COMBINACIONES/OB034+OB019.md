## 1. Combination data

**Combined operations:** [[OB_034_Collage_Manual_Con_Reescaneo_Sucesivo|OB 034 Manual Collage With Successive Rescanning]] + [[OB_019_Matriz_Tonal|OB 019 Tonal Matrix]]  
**Operational regime:** [[Lista_Combinaciones|Combinations List]]  
**Current source image:** [[OB034_step06.jpg.webp|OB034 step06.jpg.webp]]

**Operational description:**  
The combination takes the sixth rescanned state of the manual collage from OB_034 and subjects it to the tonal matrix of OB_019. The image is reduced to cell averages, quantized to a palette of up to 256 tones, and its indices are reordered. The action can cover the entire image or be limited to the bands surrounding cuts, overlaps, and folds of the collage.

## 3. Variables

**Controlled variables:**

- Columns and rows of the matrix
- Number of palette tones, up to 256
- Remapping mode: shift, explicit map, or random
- `ORDEN_A_DESORDEN`, from 0.0 (intact palette) to 10.0 (complete permutation)
- Random seed to repeat a run if desired
- Application over the complete matrix or only over scars
- Canny thresholds, radius, and blur of the bands
- Grid, dithering, and matrix reading diagrams

**Uncontrolled / accidental variables:**

- Which physical edges are recognized as a scar
- Which tones present in the collage are affected by a random permutation
- Persistence of cuts, paper shadows, figure, and background after cell averaging

## 6. Crisis and formal-perceptual reading

OB_034 opens up the figure through material decisions: cutting, shifting, overlapping, and rescanning. OB_019 reorganizes the record of those gestures as a structure of cells and tones. The scar mask allows the tonal operation to appear only where the collage reveals its physical joints; in the complete matrix, on the other hand, the regular order cuts evenly through figure, background, and matter.

The order-to-disorder parameter shifts the problem from a stable translation to a contingent chromatic redistribution: with low values, the original relationship between tone and color is preserved; with high values, that relationship is interrupted without adding outside colors to the image. The selection of the friction zone remains pending.
