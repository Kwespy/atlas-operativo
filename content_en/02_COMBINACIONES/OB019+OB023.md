## 1. Operation details

**Internal number:** OB_019 + OB_023  
**Operational regime:** [[Lista_Combinaciones|Combination List]]  
**Source image:**  Photograph and Printed Image

**Operational description:**  
The image is printed with toner. It is submerged in a tray of water. Drops of ink are gradually added using a syringe —Magenta-Green-Yellow-Blue-Black— and the process is recorded. Then, a 40/60 recognizability result is chosen to be fed into the Tonal Matrix script, which grids the image into a layout with 156 different tones. The tonal squares are then reorganized.

## 6. Crisis and formal-perceptual reading


**Where the crisis occurs and the operation affects the visual grammar of the image: form, contour, color, figure/ground relationship, legibility, recognition, and support:**

The crisis occurs with the ink floating on the water, covering and veiling the printed image at the bottom of the tray, affecting its entire visual grammar, to a greater extent color and the figure/ground relationship. With the black ink, the image becomes destabilized and begins to take on more contrast and pictorial richness. 
With the tonal script, the image enters a crisis regarding its legibility and the support; it loses track of the format in which it was produced, and by remapping the image with greater intensity, it becomes abstract, yet it gains visual richness and produces a new visual experience.
