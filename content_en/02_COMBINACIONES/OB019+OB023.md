## 1. Operation Data

**Internal number:** OB_019 + OB_023  
**Operational regime:** [[Lista_Combinaciones|Combinations List]]  
**Source image:** Photograph and printed image.

**Operational description:**  
The image is printed in toner and submerged in a tray of water. Drops of ink — magenta, green, yellow, blue, and black — are gradually added using a syringe while the process is recorded. The result is then chosen with a 40/60 level of recognizability and introduced into the tonal matrix script, which organizes the image into a grid with 156 different tones. Afterward, the tonal squares are reorganized.

## 6. Crisis and Formal-Perceptual Reading


**Where the crisis occurs and the operation affects the visual grammar of the image: shape, outline, color, figure/ground relationship, legibility, recognition, and support:**

The crisis occurs when the ink floats on the water and covers or veils the printed image at the bottom of the tray. This affects its entire visual grammar, especially color and the figure-ground relationship. With the black ink, the image becomes destabilized and acquires greater contrast and pictorial richness.
With the tonal script, the image enters a crisis regarding its legibility and support: the format in which it was produced is no longer perceived. By intensifying the remapping, the image becomes abstract; nevertheless, it gains visual richness and produces a new experience.
