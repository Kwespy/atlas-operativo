## 1. Combination Data

**Combined operations:** [[OB_025_Saturacion_vecindad_rgb|OB 025 Saturacion vecindad rgb]] + [[OB_034_Collage_Manual_Con_Reescaneo_Sucesivo|OB 034 Collage Manual Con Reescaneo Sucesivo]]

**Source image:** [[OB034_step06.jpg.webp|OB034 step06.jpg.webp]]

**Operational description:**  
The sixth scan of the collage from OB_034 passes through the operation of OB_025. The image is organized into RGB proportion families; each sufficiently large connected component is unified and saturated according to its dominant channel. The collage edges and material misalignments are subjected to more extensive color fields.

## 3. Variables

**Controlled variables:**

- Pre-smoothing that determines the extent of each RGB family
- Number of RGB bins
- Minimum area of transformed regions
- Saturation, unification, and final blending
- Subsequent global saturation and contrast
- Protection of the physical edges of the collage

**Uncontrolled / accidental variables:**

- Shape of the components produced by the shadows, cuts, and overlaps of the collage
- Dominant channel that each region inherits from the scan
- Persistence of facial features within large color fields

## 6. Crisis and Formal-Perceptual Reading

The combination confronts two scales: the cuts, repetitions, and local shadows of OB_034 against OB_025's tendency to gather chromatic zones under dominant fields. The figure may survive through remnants of eyes, hair, or outlines, but color ceases to continuously describe skin, paper, and background. The selection of a friction zone remains pending.
