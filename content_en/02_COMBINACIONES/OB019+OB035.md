**Source image:** [[OB035_step16.jpeg|OB035 step16.jpeg]]

**Operational description:**  
The combination starts from the portrait whose skin was reconstructed using body fragments by OB_035. Over this result, OB_019 reduces the image to a matrix of tonal averages, quantizes it, and shifts its palette indices. Arcimboldo's triangular topology does not disappear: it is read instead within tonal blocks of varying scale.

## 6. Crisis and Formal-Perceptual Reading

OB_035 replaces the material of the skin while maintaining the recognition anchors of the portrait. OB_019 then acts upon this substitution: it converts the differences between body fragments into discrete tone steps and redistributes their chromatic relationships. The combination creates tension between the coexistence of two grids: the anatomical triangles of the first operation and the tonal grid of the second.

With a dense matrix, the figure is still organized by its eyes, hair, and body. As columns and tones are reduced, reading no longer relies on individual body parts and shifts toward a value structure. The selection of the friction zone remains pending.
