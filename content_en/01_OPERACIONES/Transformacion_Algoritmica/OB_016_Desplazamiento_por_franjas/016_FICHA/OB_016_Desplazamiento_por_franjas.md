## 6. Crisis and formal-perceptual reading

**Main crisis:** Structure, form, and contour

**The operation affects the visual grammar of the image: form, contour, color, figure/ground relationship, legibility, recognition, and support:**

The shape and contour are the first things to be destroyed; the color remains recognizable, and the relationship between figure and ground begins to enter into crisis later on. It is at that point that legibility is lost when the figure and ground start to mix.


---

Simple operation; it is more of an image aesthetic than a structural operation of the image.
