## 1. Operation data

**Internal number:** OB_033  
**Operational regime:** [[Lista_Traduccion_Sistemas_Representacion|Representation Systems Translation List]]  
**Source image:** [[Input_001.jpg.webp|Input 001.jpg.webp]]: technical image of a ball on a glass support.

**Operational description:**  
The technical image is translated into a charcoal drawing, regulating how much of it reaches the hand. In the first drawing, the ball is represented without seeing it. In the subsequent ones, looking alternates with blind drawing: one looks for a moment, stops looking and draws, and in each new drawing one looks a little more. Each step is a different sheet.

The image stops being an image and passes through another system: perception, memory and gesture. What is drawn is not the photograph, but what remains available of it at that moment. The variable is the amount of visual information entering the translation.

---

## 2. Tools and procedure

**Tools / medium:**
- Charcoal
- Paper (one sheet per step)
- Reference technical image
- Scanner for recording

**Technical procedure:**  
1. Draw the ball without looking at the technical image.
2. On the following sheet, look at the image for a moment, stop seeing it and draw.
3. Repeat on each new sheet, slightly increasing the observation time each time.
4. Scan each sheet.

The sequence preserves 13 drawings, made on May 14, 2026 between 17:48 and 18:04.

**Script:** Not applicable.  
**Step recording:** the date and time of the scan and the original name of each file are in its image metadata (EXIF, *ImageDescription* field).

## 3. Variables

**Controlled variables:**
- Amount of observation of the technical image before each drawing, increasing throughout the sequence.
- Medium: charcoal on paper.
- A new sheet per step.

**Uncontrolled / accidental variables:**
- Exact observation time in each step: not documented.
- Charcoal smudges in the background from the wrist rubbing against the sheet.
- Pressure, speed and thickness of the stroke.
- What memory retains or invents between looking and drawing.

## 5. Visual selection

### Crisis / threshold according to AI-assisted reading

The sequence moves through the reading in the reverse direction of the other operations: it begins in the illegible and ends in the stable.

- **Steps 01–02, close to the destroyed:** several balls overlap on the same sheet, with circles that do not close, loose smudges and polygon fragments thrown toward the edges. The idea of "something round" remains, but without a fixed place or scale.
- **Steps 03–09, zone of friction:** the structure is already there (circle, support, table line), but the interior does not match the object. Pentagons appear as crossings out, rectangles or letters floating inside the sphere, and the ball tilts or deforms (step 06). Recognition works through the scheme, not through the drawing of the pattern.
- **Steps 10–13, toward the stable:** the pattern of pentagons is ordered and the ball becomes a "correct" soccer ball. In step 13 the drawing is already read as a resolved representation, the cliché of the ball.

The most fertile point appears in step 09: the object is recognized completely, but its surface is made of signs that memory could not place.

## 6. Crisis and formal-perceptual reading

**The operation affects form, contour, figure/ground relationship, legibility and recognition:**

The first thing to stabilize is not the object, but its scheme: the circle, the support and the table line appear very early and remain throughout the sequence. The last thing to arrive is the specific: the pentagon pattern, which is what makes the ball that ball and not just any sphere. Between those two moments, the image exists as structure without content: one knows what it is, but not what it looks like.

The contour is constructed by repetition: several overlapping circles search for a single edge that does not exist at first. In the first steps that search remains visible and the drawing shows the process of searching for the form rather than the form itself. The background is not empty: the smudges from the wrist rubbing register the body that drew, a trace that the technical image did not have.

## 7. Final evaluation and artistic reference

**Operation notes / what I learned:**  
In this operation, crisis is reached by reducing information, not by destroying it: what is controlled is how much of the image reaches the hand. The order in which the image is reconstructed (first the scheme, then the detail) shows which parts of an image memory retains and which ones it needs to look at again.
